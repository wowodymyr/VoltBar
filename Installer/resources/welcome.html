VoltBar ⚡

Menu bar undervolting for Intel Haswell / Broadwell Macs, with its own MSR driver VoltBarMSR.kext.

This installs:

  • VoltBar.app in /Applications (menu bar app)
  • a background service com.voltbar.daemon that applies your profile at boot and after sleep
  • the command-line tool /usr/local/bin/voltbarctl
  • the driver VoltBarMSR.kext in /Library/Application Support/VoltBar (loaded by the service)

On first use macOS may ask you to allow the VoltBarMSR extension in System Settings → Privacy & Security and restart. Unsigned kernel extensions must be permitted by SIP (as set up by OpenCore Legacy Patcher).

If VoltageShift's own launch daemon is installed, it will be disabled so the two don't conflict (a backup is kept in /Library/Application Support/VoltBar).

Warning: wrong voltage settings can freeze the system, and overvolting can damage the CPU. Use at your own risk.
